## Primary Font Family

The entire design uses **Inter** as the primary and only font family.[1]

## Font Weights Used

- **400 (Regular)**: Default body text and secondary content
- **500 (Medium)**: Titles, input labels, and emphasized text
- **600 (Semibold)**: Navigation tabs, buttons, headings, and prominent UI elements

## Typography Hierarchy

### Headings & Titles
- **16px Medium (500)**: Section headings like "Settings & Privacy", "Email Address", "Change Password" with `line-height: 20px` and `letter-spacing: -0.006em`[1]
- **14px Semibold (600)**: Tab labels ("Account & Security", "Linked Accounts", "Data & Privacy") with `line-height: 150%` and `letter-spacing: -0.04em` or `-0.02em`[1]
- **14px Medium (500)**: Sidebar menu items (selected state), form field labels with `line-height: 120%` or `20px`[1]

### Body Text
- **14px Regular (400)**: Input placeholder text, sidebar menu items (default state), general body text with `line-height: 150%` or `120%` and `letter-spacing: -0.02em`[1]
- **12px Regular (400)**: Breadcrumbs, helper text, descriptions with `line-height: 120%` (14px)[1]
- **12px Medium (500)**: Emphasized breadcrumb items like "Settings & Privacy" with `line-height: 120%`[1]

### Button Text
- **14px Semibold (600)**: Primary button text with `line-height: 120%`[1]

## Line Heights

- **120%**: Most compact text elements (navigation, buttons, breadcrumbs)[1]
- **150%**: More readable body text and tab labels[1]
- **Fixed values (20px, 22px)**: Used for consistent alignment in form fields and titles[1]

## Letter Spacing

- **-0.006em**: Standard for 16px and 14px medium titles[1]
- **-0.02em**: Used for 14px regular body text and some tabs[1]
- **-0.04em**: Tighter spacing for emphasized 14px semibold text and some headings[1]

- #FFFFFF: Button text on colored backgrounds[1]
